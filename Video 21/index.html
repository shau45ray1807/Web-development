Specificity Calculation:

  • Universal Selector: 10
  • Element or Pseudo-element Selector: 10
  • Class or Attribute or Pseudo-class Selector: 10
  • ID Selector: 100
  • Inline Styles: 1000

    • So, the order of specificity is:

    Inline Style > ID Slector > Class Selector > Element Selector

    "!important" declarations have higher specificity compared to other declarations.
